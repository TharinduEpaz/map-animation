| Mode | What the viewer sees | Useful for | Effort in this app |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Country Highlight** | Camera approaches a country, its territory fills with color, its name appears | Country introductions, geography, news | Medium |
| **Location Spotlight** | Zoom into one city or landmark; reveal a pin and title | Travel intros, event locations, business videos | Low |
| **Multi-stop Journey** | Route draws through several locations, pausing at each | Travel itineraries, road trips, expeditions | Medium |
| **Country Comparison** | Two or more countries highlighted in different colors with names and optional facts | Geography comparisons, sports, economic explainers | Medium |
| **Country Countdown** | Countries appear one at a time with a rank and caption | “Five places to visit,” rankings, list videos | Medium |
| **Region Highlight** | A state, province, district, or custom area is outlined and filled | Local news, regional explainers, tourism | Medium–high |
| **Connections / Flow Map** | Multiple animated lines connect a central location to other places | Airline networks, trade, migration, business reach | Medium |
| **Data Map** | Countries change color or display growing circles according to values | Population, tourism, statistics | Medium–high |
| **Historical Timeline** | Territories and labels change as a date counter advances | History, territorial change, documentary content | High |
